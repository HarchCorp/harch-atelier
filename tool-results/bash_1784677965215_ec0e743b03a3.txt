## Phase 1 — Reasoning about what a HarchCorp LEGAL COUNSEL really needs

A HarchCorp in-house legal counsel (L. Reyes, Legal & Regulatory Monitor) has a fundamentally different lens than the trader (markets), admin (platform health), or analyst (event triage). They live in **regulatory exposure + legal liability + preservation obligations**. The Palantir-grade mandate "beaucoup beaucoup de graphique" applies — every section needs dense tables (registers/dockets) + charts (gauges/radars/heatmaps/timelines).

What the legal counsel owns, in priority order:

1. **Regulatory register & filing calendar** — Morocco-specific statutory deadlines (AMMC quarterly disclosures + insider-dealing declarations + Loi 17-23 ESG chapter; Bank Al-Maghrib prudential reporting; BCBS Basel III LCR/NSFR; AMO/CNSS social filings; CNPDP Loi 09-08 data-protection register + 72h breach notification; OFAC/EU/UN sanctions screening; WCO customs). Missing a filing = fine + reputational damage. They need a register (sortable/filterable/searchable), a regulator × status heatmap, an upcoming-filing timeline, and per-obligation risk weighting.

2. **Cyber incident posture** — when a breach occurs, counsel's role is NOT to fix it but to ensure **statutory disclosure obligations are met** (Loi 09-08 72h notification to CNPDP, GDPR Article 33 for EU ops) and the **evidence chain is preserved** for regulator inquiry. They need: threat intel feed (MITRE ATT&CK mapped), 30-day incident trend, posture gauge, readiness radar (8 dimensions), CVE exposure list with patch status.

3. **Financial-risk legal exposure** — counterparty defaults trigger cross-default clauses, ISDA/CSA disputes. They need: credit/liquidity/market/operational gauges, VaR trend vs limit, counterparty table with ISDA + cross-default + utilization, liquidity ratio bars (LCR/NSFR/MLA/Tier-1). Moroccan counterparties: Attijariwafa, BMCE, CFG Bank, CIH, Banque Populaire, OCP, IAM.

4. **ESG disclosure posture** — Loi 17-23 + AMMC ESG chapter for listed issuers, plus TCFD/GRI/CDP/CSRD/UNGC/ILO for cross-border ops. They need: E/S/G score bars + sub-dimension radar, 12-month carbon emissions trend (Scope 1/2/3), controversy feed, disclosure register with status + due date.

5. **Geopolitical/sanctions exposure** — Law 43-05 on financial sanctions compliance. They need: entity × sanctioned-jurisdiction heatmap (8 jurisdictions: Iran/Russia/Syria/DPRK/Cuba/Venezuela/Myanmar/Belarus with direct/indirect exposure), regional risk index cards (Morocco/EU/US/MENA/SSA/APAC), trade-policy timeline (tariffs/export controls/sanctions/agreements/quotas).

6. **Reputational legal risk** — defamation, executive reputation, brand controversies that may trigger cease-and-desist or litigation. They need: reputation index gauge + 30-day trend, executive reputation cards (6 execs with sentiment/delta/mentions/negative%), stakeholder sentiment stacked bars (7 stakeholder groups), brand controversy feed.

7. **Legal matters docket** — the bread-and-butter: every open matter (Litigation/Arbitration/Regulatory Inquiry/IP/Contract/Compliance/Investigation/Employment/Real Estate), with counterparty, external counsel firm, budget vs burn, next milestone, deadline, exposure. Matters-by-status donut + budget vs burn bar + upcoming milestones timeline.

8. **Litigation hold register** — when litigation is reasonably anticipated, counsel MUST issue a hold notice preserving all relevant ESI (FRCP Rule 37(e) safe harbor in US; equivalent civil procedure in MA/EU). Failure = spoliation + adverse inference. They need: hold register (matter, custodians, data sources preserved, status, last reminder), custodian coverage bars by data source, hold-status timeline (90d weekly: active/released/superseded).

Morocco-aware design decisions baked into the data layer:
- Real regulators: AMMC, Bank Al-Maghrib, BCBS, AMO, CNSS, CNPDP, OFAC, EU, UN, WCO.
- Real Moroccan counterparties: Attijariwafa, BMCE, CFG Bank, CIH, Banque Populaire, OCP, IAM, Marocaine de Distribution.
- Real external counsel firms: Clifford Chance (Paris), Gide Loyrette Nouel (Casablanca), Baker McKenzie (London), Naciri & Associés (Rabat), Allen & Overy (Paris), K&L Gates (NY), CMS Camozzi (Milan), Latham & Watkins (Brussels).
- Real Morocco-relevant statutes: Loi 09-08 (data protection + 72h breach notification), Loi 17-23 (AMMC ESG chapter), Law 43-05 (sanctions compliance), Sapin II-style anti-corruption for EU ops.
- Real Moroccan outlets: L'Économiste, Medias24, TelQuel, Forbes Afrique, Le Monde.
- Tangier Free Zone export volumes (WCO customs declarations).
- Color palette: violet-700 (legal tint) primary; emerald/amber/rose/sky/violet/teal/slate secondaries. Zero indigo/blue as primary.

A HarchCorp Head of Communications / PR manager (P. Novak, PR & Comms) lives in **reputation + sentiment + share-of-voice + campaign ROI + press relations + social listening**. Unlike the trader (markets), admin (platform health), or legal counsel (regulatory exposure), the PR lead wakes up every morning asking: "What is the world saying about us today, who is saying it, and how do we shape the narrative?" The Palantir-grade mandate "beaucoup beaucoup de graphique" applies — every section needs dense charts (gauges/donuts/radars/heatmaps/scatters) + feed tables.

What the comms team owns, in priority order:

1. **Reputation score (single source of truth)** — a composite net reputation index (0–100) that rolls up everything: stakeholder sentiment, exec sentiment, controversy volume, media tone. They need a gauge + 12-month trend + 30-day trend + stakeholder × month heatmap + NPS proxy gauge + exec reputation cards + drivers/pain-points table. This is the "are we winning or losing the public-narrative war" gauge.

2. **Sentiment across outlets + social** — not just volume but tone. They need 12-month positive/neutral/negative trend (reuse/extend `sentiment12m` shape but add neutral), sentiment-by-outlet-tier stacked bars (tier-1 press is harsher than tier-3), sentiment-by-language donut (FR/AR/EN — Morocco has French-speaking press like L'Économiste + Arabic outlets like Hespress + English wires like Reuters), sentiment-by-channel radar (print/online/social/broadcast — social is always more negative), and a sentiment-drivers table that ranks topics by sentiment impact.

3. **Share of voice vs competitors** — HarchCorp vs Northwind, Vela Dynamics, Orbital Systems, Kessler & Vale, Atlas Holdings. They need a donut (overall), a 30-day trend (multi-line so they can see if a competitor is gaining), owned vs earned vs shared media split (bars), SoV by tier (stacked — tier-1 is where the brand battle is won), and a competitor mention tracker table (mentions, reach, sentiment share, SoV %, delta 30d) where HarchCorp is the highlighted row.

4. **Coverage feed** — article-level data: headline, outlet, tier, date, sentiment, reach, language, region, author. They need filter chips by tier/sentiment/language + search + sortable columns. Plus a 30-day coverage volume area chart (positive/neutral/negative stacked — with crisis-spike detection) and a top-outlets-by-reach horizontal bar chart. Morocco-aware outlets: Le Matin, L'Économiste, Aujourd'hui le Maroc, Medias24, TelQuel, Hespress, La Vie Éco, Les Inspirations ÉCO, Challenge.ma, Morocco World News, Le360, plus Reuters/Bloomberg/FT/WSJ/Le Monde.

5. **Campaign tracking** — active PR campaigns with real ROI math. They need campaign cards (name, channel, status, reach, engagement, sentiment lift, SoV lift, cost, ROI), reach-vs-cost horizontal bars, ROI scatter (cost × engagement, bubble = sentiment lift), and a 120-day Gantt-style campaign timeline. ROI = (sentimentLift × 8_000 + sovLift × 12_000 + engagement × 4) / cost — a benefit/cost ratio.

6. **Reputation deep-dive** — beyond the overview gauge: 12-month trend line, 30-day trend line, stakeholder × month heatmap (7 stakeholders × 6 months, color-coded 28–88), NPS proxy gauge (-100..+100 mapped to 0..100 arc), reputation drivers/pain points table (sortable), executive reputation mini-cards (6 execs with sentiment bars + deltas + mentions + negative%).

7. **Press release library** — draft + scheduled + published + embargoed releases with distribution list, pickups, outlets reached, sentiment. They need a status donut, a pickup-by-outlet horizontal bar chart, a sortable/filterable release library table, and a draft-editor preview card (visual only — Input/Textarea/Button shadcn primitives wired to read-only state).

8. **Social listening real-time stream** — 15+ mentions across 6 platforms (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok) with author, handle, content snippet, timestamp, reach, engagement, sentiment, virality score. They need mentions-by-platform bars, virality 7-day timeline (total + high-virality subset), a hashtag cloud (sized by volume, color-coded by sentiment), top influencers table, and the real-time mention stream table with platform filter chips + sortable columns.

(7) The `campaignRoi()` function uses a simplified benefit formula (sentimentLift × 8_000 + sovLift × 12_000 + engagement × 4) / cost — a real ROI model would weight these by industry benchmarks and customer LTV data.

(2) Market accent is amber-700 across all 5 sections for role identity (vs legal=violet, admin=rotating, trader=emerald, pr=rose). (3) All tables use shadcn `<Table>` primitives with `max-h-[N] overflow-y-auto harch-scroll` for long lists. (4) All charts use recharts via `DeferredChart` to avoid ResponsiveContainer flash. (5) The entities dataset REUSES `moroccanEquities` + `BvcSector` from `./market-data` and EXTENDS them with private Moroccan corporates + peers + counterparties (no duplication). (6) The dataset is fully self-contained — no `any`, deterministic via mulberry32 seeded (seed 20251118) so first paint is stable. (9) The peer ranking bars overlay a thin vertical risk marker (positioned left-right by risk score, colored by severity) on top of the revenue-share bar — gives a compact "is this peer's risk proportional to its revenue?" visual. (10) The watchlist card sparkline uses recharts `AreaChart` with a per-card gradient id (`wlsGrad-${signal.id}`) — small enough that no axes are needed. (2) The peer benchmark table's ROE calculation is a simplified heuristic (`netIncome / (assets - mktCap)`) — for a real production deployment this would come from a financial-data vendor (Bloomberg / FactSet) and would include proper shareholder-equity adjustments. (3) The watchlist sparkline is a 20-point series with no axes — for hover precision a real sparkline would show a tooltip; here it's purely decorative pulse-visual. (4) The 41-entity directory is intentionally compact (one page); for a real production deployment with hundreds of entities, server-side pagination + virtual scrolling would be needed (the current `max-h-[560px] overflow-y-auto` works for 41 but not 4000). (5) The ownership pie uses a fixed 5-color palette (amber/sky/emerald/violet/slate) — entities with > 5 shareholders would cycle colors; current data caps at 4 shareholders per entity so this isn't hit.
